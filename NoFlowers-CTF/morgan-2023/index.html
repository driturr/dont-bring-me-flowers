Weekend in the cabin

We took a trip. Just a weekend away. First time we'd had that kind of time together, uninterrupted. No rushing home. No checking the clock. Everything felt just right. She looked at me differently when we were alone like that. Like she was seeing me for the first time. I felt it too.

[January 18, 2023]

I think I like her for real

We were at her place. She put on music and just... danced. Not for me, just for herself. Completely unselfconscious. I couldn't look away. When she noticed me watching, instead of stopping, she pulled me up. We moved together in her kitchen at midnight. It was the simplest thing and also everything.

[February 24, 2023]

8-M Omg...!

We went to the protest together. It hit me halfway there that people would probably notice us now... like.. it wasn’t something we could really hide anymore!

There were so many people when we got there, and it felt so hopeful and powerful! All of us together, shouting along with the chants.

“Trans rights are human rights!” kept coming up again and again, loud enough that it echoed through everything.

I remember looking at her and thinking... it's quite obvious we're together...
